Built for people with a few hundred PDFs and a phone, a laptop, and a browser tab they keep losing books between.

Your place follows you

Reading position, highlights, notes and bookmarks sync across devices. Offline, it keeps them on the device and sends them when you're back online.

Tabs, like a browser

Keep several books open and switch instantly. "Open with" from Explorer drops a book into a new tab.

PDF and real EPUB

EPUBs reflow as ebooks, with chapters and adjustable type. They aren't flattened into PDFs.

Notion to book

Paste a Notion link and get a typeset PDF, subpages and all. Import it again later to pull in your latest edits.

Edit Arena

Reorder, rotate, crop, merge and split pages. Every edit is a new version, so you can always go back.

Friends and shared shelves

Send a book to a friend, see what they're reading, and keep private shelves private.

Book clubs, without the spoilers.

Start a club with friends or join a public one. Read the same books, see where everyone is, and talk chapter by chapter. Nothing past your page is ever shown to you.

Many books, in order

A club reads one book now, lines up the next, and keeps the finished ones. Series stay together, from book one to seven, each with its own talk.

Spoiler-safe by design

Tell the club how far you've read. Chapters, posts and reactions past that point stay locked until you get there.

Chat by chapter

A room for the whole club, for each series, for each book and for every chapter. React to the chapters that wrecked you.

Debate, don't just comment

Hot takes, theories that get settled, character vs character, book vs book. Vote, argue a side, switch if you're convinced.

Everyone's highlights, if you want them

Members' highlights and notes gather in one place. Your book stays clean, unless you choose to see everyone's colours or just a few people's.

Your reader card

Characters loved and hated, hot takes, favourite trope, reading personality and badges. Share it as a picture.

Private clubs share books only when their organisers confirm they have the right to, and can keep them inside the app. Public clubs share conversation, never files.

Made for reading, not for scrolling past.

SynKitab started as a way to stop losing books between devices. It grew into a reader that typesets Notion pages, reflows EPUBs, lets you edit pages safely, and keeps your place across a phone, a PC and a browser.

"Kitab" is Arabic, Urdu and Hindi for book. The rest is sync.
